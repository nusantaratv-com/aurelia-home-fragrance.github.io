OUR STORY

Fragrance should feel personal.

AURELIA was imagined around a simple idea: scent can change the atmosphere of a space in seconds. Our collections pair familiar notes with calm, considered objects so everyday rooms can feel more intentional.

We focus on warm materials, clear fragrance families and giftable presentation. The result is a collection designed to be used, shared and remembered.
