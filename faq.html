FAQ

Answers to common AURELIA shopping questions.

How should I choose a scent?

Start with the room and mood: fresh for bright spaces, floral for softer rooms, woody for depth and amber for cozy evenings.

Can I combine fragrances?

Yes. Layering compatible scent families can create a more personal atmosphere. Start lightly and build gradually.

Are the products giftable?

Gift sets are designed for easy gifting, and support can help with choosing a collection.
